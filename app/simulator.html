streamline-telco-prod › Save Desk solution

Save Desk: churn early warning for Streamline Telco

One governed journey from raw data to the retention agent's screen, on the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform. Click any step.
The business problem

~16.8% of postpaid subscribers leave each year. Customers say it on care calls, the network degrades, bills jump, but the signals sit in three systems and retention reacts only at the porting request.

What runs every night at 02:00

Lakeflow ingests → AI Functions read every call → MLflow model scores every subscriber → Lakebase serves the save list → agents act in the Databricks App; managers ask Genie. All governed by Unity Catalog, deployed as one Asset Bundle.

Read every care call with AI Functions

AI Functions call Databricks-hosted foundation models straight from SQL, governed by Unity Catalog. No model to host; transcripts never leave the platform. Only new calls are processed each night.
INSERT INTO streamline_telco.silver.care_calls_enriched
SELECT c.call_id, c.subscriber_id, c.transcript,
  ai_analyze_sentiment(c.transcript)                                                   AS sentiment,
  ai_classify(c.transcript, ARRAY('network','billing','retention','device','general')) AS intent,
  COALESCE(TRY_CAST(ai_query('databricks-meta-llama-3-3-70b-instruct',
     CONCAT('Does this care call show intent to leave, port the number or switch provider? Answer 1 or 0: ', c.transcript))
  AS INT), 0)                                                                         AS switch_intent
FROM streamline_telco.silver.care_calls c
LEFT ANTI JOIN streamline_telco.silver.care_calls_enriched e ON c.call_id = e.call_id;
