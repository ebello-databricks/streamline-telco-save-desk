01 The business problem

In deregulated energy, switching is one click — and Plenitude finds out too late.

Italy's retail energy market is fully liberalised. A comparator site and a five-minute form is all it takes to leave. The buying signals — a bill shock, an unresolved complaint, an "I'm calling the competition" — arrive first through the contact centre, as raw audio that no model ever sees.

  • ~2.5MB2C power & gas customers in scope — residential and micro-business across Italy.
  • ~12%leave every year — roughly 300,000 customers lost annually, each taking their margin and CLV with them.
  • 0of that voice signal reaches a model. Calls are handled reactively, ticket by ticket; churn intent is never scored, ranked, or acted on before renewal.

08 Decisions & trade-offs

Every choice was deliberate — here's the why.

Gradient boosting, not deep learning

Why: tabular data at this scale, and the retention team needs explainable reasons per customer. GBM gives strong AUC + feature attributions the business trusts. A neural net would add opacity for no lift here.

FMAPI (ai_query), not a bespoke fine-tune

Why: time-to-value. ai_analyze_sentiment / ai_classify run in SQL today with governance built in. A custom transcript model is a later optimisation, not a v1 dependency.

Lakebase for serving, not batch-only

Why: the cockpit needs low-latency reads of the latest scores as agents work the list. Lakebase serves operationally while the analytics stay in the lakehouse — one platform, no export.

Synthetic data, generated for signal

Why: customer-safe by construction — no real Plenitude data ever touches the repo. Churn is generated from real-world drivers so the model learns genuine, defensible patterns.

AI as a force multiplier: the whole build — data generator, pipeline, model, app, deck — was produced with Claude Code as a pair-builder; the architecture decisions, the value framing, and the trade-offs above are the author's.
